Title: Comparative Effectiveness of Antihypertensive Medications
Source: Based on NICE NG136, ONTARGET trial, JNC8 guidelines

Hypertension affects 1.28 billion adults worldwide. First-line agents:

ACE INHIBITORS (ramipril, lisinopril, enalapril):
- Preferred in: diabetes + CKD (eGFR >30), post-MI, heart failure
- Mechanism: block angiotensin-converting enzyme, reduce angiotensin II
- Key side effect: dry cough (10-15%), hyperkalaemia
- Contraindicated: pregnancy (all trimesters), bilateral renal artery stenosis
- AVOID combining with ARBs (ONTARGET trial showed increased harm)

ARBs (losartan, valsartan, candesartan):
- Use when ACE inhibitor cough occurs — same renal protective profile
- Preferred in: diabetic nephropathy, heart failure with reduced EF
- Contraindicated: pregnancy, bilateral renal artery stenosis

CALCIUM CHANNEL BLOCKERS (amlodipine, nifedipine):
- Preferred in: elderly patients, African/Caribbean descent, angina
- Mechanism: block L-type calcium channels, reduce peripheral vascular resistance
- Side effects: peripheral oedema, flushing, headache
- Safe in pregnancy (nifedipine)

THIAZIDE-LIKE DIURETICS (indapamide, chlorthalidone):
- First-line uncomplicated hypertension
- Monitor: hypokalaemia, hyponatraemia, hyperuricaemia
- Preferred in: heart failure, elderly, Black patients

BETA-BLOCKERS (bisoprolol, carvedilol, metoprolol):
- NOT first-line for uncomplicated hypertension
- Indicated: post-MI, heart failure with reduced EF, rate control in AF
- Avoid in: asthma, COPD (use with caution), decompensated heart failure

TREATMENT TARGETS:
- General: <140/90 mmHg
- Diabetes or CKD: <130/80 mmHg
- Elderly (>80 years): <150/90 mmHg (HYVET trial)

COMBINATION THERAPY:
- Step 1: ACEi/ARB or CCB
- Step 2: ACEi/ARB + CCB
- Step 3: Add thiazide-like diuretic
- Step 4 (resistant HTN): Add spironolactone 25mg (PATHWAY-2 trial)
