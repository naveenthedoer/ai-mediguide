Title: Pharmacological Management of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus
Source: Based on ADA Standards of Care 2024, EMPA-REG, LEADER, DAPA-CKD trials

DIAGNOSIS:
- HbA1c ≥48 mmol/mol (6.5%) on two occasions
- Fasting glucose ≥7.0 mmol/L
- 2hr OGTT glucose ≥11.1 mmol/L

TREATMENT ALGORITHM:

Step 1 — Metformin (first-line unless contraindicated):
- Contraindicated: eGFR <30, hepatic impairment, contrast procedures
- Hold 48h before/after iodinated contrast media
- Side effects: GI upset (take with food), B12 deficiency long-term
- Dose: start 500mg BD, titrate to 1g BD over 4 weeks

Step 2 — Add second agent based on comorbidities:

IF established CVD or high CV risk:
- GLP-1 receptor agonist (semaglutide, liraglutide, dulaglutide)
  → LEADER trial: liraglutide reduced CV death by 22%
  → Once weekly SC injection (semaglutide) or daily (liraglutide)
  → Additional benefit: 3-5kg weight loss
- OR SGLT2 inhibitor (empagliflozin, dapagliflozin, canagliflozin)
  → EMPA-REG: empagliflozin reduced CV death by 38%
  → Also reduces hospitalisation for heart failure

IF CKD (eGFR 25-60):
- Dapagliflozin (DAPA-CKD trial: 39% reduction in renal progression)
- Avoid SGLT2 inhibitors if eGFR <25 (reduced efficacy)

IF obesity (BMI >30):
- GLP-1 RA preferred — greatest weight loss benefit
- Semaglutide 2.4mg weekly (SUSTAIN-6 trial)

IF cost is primary concern:
- Sulphonylurea (gliclazide MR preferred over glibenclamide)
- Risk: hypoglycaemia, weight gain
- Gliclazide MR lower hypoglycaemia risk than older sulphonylureas

MONITORING:
- HbA1c every 3 months until stable, then 6-monthly
- Target: <53 mmol/mol (7%) for most; individualise for frail/elderly
- eGFR annually (or more often if CKD)
- Urine ACR annually (early nephropathy detection)
- Annual retinal screening, foot check, BP measurement

SICK DAY RULES:
- Hold metformin during vomiting/diarrhoea/dehydration
- Hold SGLT2 inhibitors perioperatively (DKA risk)
- Never stop insulin in type 1 diabetes

HYPOGLYCAEMIA MANAGEMENT:
- Mild-moderate: 15-20g fast-acting glucose (glucose tablets, 150ml juice)
- Severe (unconscious): glucagon IM/SC or IV dextrose 10%
- Recheck glucose 15 minutes after treatment
