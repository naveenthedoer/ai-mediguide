Title: High-Risk Drug Interactions in Primary Care
Source: Based on BNF, MHRA, Stockley's Drug Interactions

METFORMIN:
- Iodinated contrast media: hold 48h before and after; risk of lactic acidosis via AKI
- NSAIDs + ACEi/ARB + diuretic (triple whammy): acute kidney injury risk; hold metformin
- Alcohol (binge drinking): lactic acidosis risk; counsel patients

STATINS (simvastatin, atorvastatin):
- Amiodarone + simvastatin >20mg: myopathy/rhabdomyolysis risk; cap simvastatin at 20mg
- Clarithromycin/erythromycin + simvastatin: CYP3A4 inhibition; switch to pravastatin
- Grapefruit juice: CYP3A4 inhibition; avoid with simvastatin/atorvastatin
- Fluconazole: rhabdomyolysis risk; suspend statin during short antifungal course

SSRIs (fluoxetine, paroxetine, sertraline):
- MAOIs: CONTRAINDICATED — serotonin syndrome (14-day washout required)
- Tramadol: serotonin syndrome risk; avoid combination
- Lithium: additive serotonin effect; monitor lithium levels
- Tamoxifen + fluoxetine/paroxetine: CYP2D6 inhibition reduces tamoxifen efficacy
  → Use sertraline or citalopram instead in breast cancer patients

ACE INHIBITORS + ARBs:
- NEVER combine (ONTARGET trial): increased AKI, hyperkalaemia, hypotension
- Potassium-sparing diuretics (spironolactone, amiloride): hyperkalaemia risk
- NSAIDs: reduce antihypertensive effect + AKI risk
- Lithium: ACEi reduces lithium excretion; toxic levels possible

ANTIBIOTICS:
- Quinolones (ciprofloxacin) + antacids/iron: chelation reduces absorption; separate by 2h
- Metronidazole + alcohol: disulfiram-like reaction (flushing, vomiting, tachycardia)
- Rifampicin + oral contraceptives: enzyme induction reduces contraceptive efficacy
- Clarithromycin + simvastatin: see statins above
