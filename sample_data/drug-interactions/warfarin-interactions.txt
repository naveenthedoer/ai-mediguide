Title: Warfarin Drug Interactions — Clinical Reference
Source: Based on BNF, Stockley's Drug Interactions, MHRA guidance

MECHANISM: Vitamin K antagonist — inhibits clotting factors II, VII, IX, X
THERAPEUTIC RANGE: INR 2.0-3.0 (AF, DVT, PE); INR 2.5-3.5 (mechanical heart valve)

DRUGS THAT INCREASE INR (bleeding risk):

CYP2C9 inhibitors (major pathway for warfarin metabolism):
- Amiodarone: INR can double within 2 weeks; reduce warfarin dose 30-50%
- Fluconazole/metronidazole: significant INR elevation; avoid or monitor closely
- Ciprofloxacin: reduces Vitamin K-producing gut flora + CYP inhibition
- Co-trimoxazole: potent interaction; check INR within 3 days of starting

Antiplatelet/anticoagulant additive risk:
- Aspirin >100mg/day: additive bleeding; avoid unless benefit outweighs risk
- NSAIDs (ibuprofen, naproxen, diclofenac): platelet inhibition + GI mucosal damage
- SSRIs: inhibit platelet 5-HT2A receptors; additive bleeding risk

DRUGS THAT DECREASE INR (clot risk):

CYP2C9 inducers:
- Rifampicin: most potent inducer; INR can halve within 5-7 days of starting
- Carbamazepine/phenytoin: significant induction; monitor weekly when starting
- St John's Wort: OTC herbal — patients often do not report; always ask

MONITORING RULES:
- Check INR within 3-5 days of adding/stopping ANY interacting drug
- Re-check at 1 week and 1 month after any dose change
- Educate patients: consistent Vitamin K diet, report new medications
- Signs of over-anticoagulation: unusual bruising, prolonged bleeding, blood in urine/stool
