Title: Acute Chest Pain Assessment and Management
Source: Based on NICE NG185, ESC NSTEMI Guidelines 2020, Surviving Sepsis

IMMEDIATE ASSESSMENT — first 10 minutes:
1. 12-lead ECG (within 10 minutes of presentation)
2. IV access + bloods: FBC, U&E, troponin (high-sensitivity), coagulation, lipids, glucose
3. Observations: BP both arms, HR, SpO2, RR, temperature
4. Brief focused history: character, onset, radiation, associated symptoms

RED FLAGS requiring immediate intervention:
- ST elevation ≥1mm in ≥2 contiguous leads → STEMI: activate PPCI pathway immediately
- New LBBB with chest pain → treat as STEMI
- Haemodynamic instability (SBP <90, HR >100)
- Signs of acute pulmonary oedema
- Suspected aortic dissection: tearing pain to back, BP differential >20mmHg between arms

HEART SCORE for NSTEMI risk stratification:
H - History (2=highly suspicious, 1=moderately, 0=slightly suspicious)
E - ECG (2=significant ST deviation, 1=LBBB/LVH/repolarisation, 0=normal)
A - Age (2=≥65, 1=45-64, 0=<45)
R - Risk factors (2=≥3 or DM, 1=1-2 factors, 0=none known)
T - Troponin (2=>3× normal, 1=1-3× normal, 0=≤normal)

Score 0-3: Low risk (1.7% MACE) → discharge with outpatient follow-up
Score 4-6: Moderate risk (12% MACE) → admit, serial troponins at 0h and 3h
Score 7-10: High risk (50% MACE) → urgent cardiology, invasive strategy

NON-CARDIAC CAUSES (exclude ACS first):
- Musculoskeletal: Tietze's syndrome, costochondritis (reproduced by palpation)
- GI: GORD, oesophageal spasm (relieved by antacids/GTN)
- Pulmonary: pneumothorax (sudden onset, pleuritic, reduced breath sounds)
- PE: pleuritic chest pain, dyspnoea, haemoptysis, risk factors (Wells score)
- Aortic dissection: tearing, maximal at onset, radiates to back

INITIAL TREATMENT (ACS confirmed):
- Aspirin 300mg stat (loading dose)
- P2Y12 inhibitor (ticagrelor 180mg or clopidogrel 300mg) after cardiology review
- Anticoagulation: fondaparinux 2.5mg SC (OASIS-5 trial preferred over UFH)
- Oxygen only if SpO2 <94%
- GTN sublingual for pain (avoid if SBP <90 or right ventricular infarct)
- Morphine 2-5mg IV if pain not controlled (monitor for nausea, respiratory depression)
